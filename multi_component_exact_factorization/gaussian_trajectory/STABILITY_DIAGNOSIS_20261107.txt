Gaussian trajectory stability investigation (2026-11-07)
========================================================

STATUS: PARTIAL NUMERICAL REPAIR, NOT A 10--40 fs VALIDATION
No coupled Gaussian CTMQC/FSSH 10 fs or 40 fs result is certified here.
Neither norm preservation nor successful process completion implies accuracy.
Original calculations, model defaults and checkpoints have not been replaced.

Source contract
---------------
Read the complete paper/MCEF_Gaussian_Trajectory_Codex_Prompt.md,
MCEF_Gaussian_Trajectory_Codex_Reference.pdf/.tex, 11_3.pdf, and the
16-page handwritten CTMQC/MCEF source. There is no sources/ directory locally.
P3 uses r=Re(C^dagger F), Cdot=(F-C*r)/Lambda. Do NOT replace r by
the projected Gaussian amplitude derivative. P8--P14 use the amplitude
metric/SVD; P15 gives Gdot_actual=F+(Lambda_dot_projected-r)*C on support.
The extra P15 term can alter observables even if total norm remains one.
The source permits documented support tests, not an unreported force,
phase-extension rule or a claim that frozen tails equal the original EOM.

What was reproduced
-------------------
The old full-channel PEN Gaussian CTMQC/FSSH pilot (K=1, Ntraj=7,
q32[-14,14), omega=.17, g=.01, DSE off, dt=.0025 au) rejected a proposal
near .241526 fs. The largest PNC error was at q=-14 and relative density
about 6e-15. Integrated norm error was about 2e-15; density-weighted PNC
error about 3e-15. A scalar norm error without its location was misleading.

Off the constraint C^dagger C=1, the direct P3 extension has
    d(C^dagger C)/dt = 2 (r/Lambda) (1-C^dagger C).
At the actual worst points the radial eigenvalue -2r/Lambda was NEGATIVE
(roughly -185 to -570 au^-1), i.e. strongly damped/stiff, not proof of
exponential radial growth. Its scale was much larger than the physical
tangent rate. There is also severe tail nonnormality in Lambda^-1 T_q Lambda:
the measured initial FFT tangent-Jacobian 2-norm was about 5.1e5 while the
largest imaginary eigenvalue magnitude was about 8.1.

Repairs and explicit diagnostic options
--------------------------------------
1. sphere_integrator.py: opt-in sphere-rk4. C=Y/||Y|| and
   Ydot=||Y|| Cdot give a homogeneous-coordinate extension of the SAME
   tangent P3 equation. All Gaussian parameters, heavy variables and force
   histories use the same RK stages. Initial invalid PNC and non-tangent
   RHS are refused, not projected away. Auxiliary norm defects are guarded
   and recorded. Physical G is never renormalized to disguise its error.
   This improves constraint integration; it is NOT a closure fix.
2. q_operators.py: optional local Hermitian fd2/fd4, FFT default unchanged.
   Action, complete internal diagonalization, kinetic energy and photon p^2
   use the same operator. These are DIFFERENT finite-grid discretizations.
   Continuum current/field estimators do not obey exact discrete product rules.
   Sampled-vacuum N bias and commutator defects are reported, not subtracted.
3. internal_channels.py: identical complete-channel matrix algebra with
   local derivative action and real sign transport when applicable. No
   eigenstate truncation, different force or hopping rule. Short comparisons
   to the previous dense calculation agree to rounding precision.
4. run.py/channel_run.py: separate integrated norm, density-weighted PNC,
   worst point/density, accepted/saved/rejected times and auxiliary defects.
   Failure does not overwrite the last valid checkpoint.
   Snapshot/save/render failures have their own phase and do not pretend to
   be rejected propagation steps. Durable saved-step advances only after
   checkpoint atomic replacement, with an injected-write-failure regression.
5. stability_validation.py: same configuration through increasing targets;
   stop on the first rejection. Saved partial frames can still be rendered.
   COMPLETE_NOT_CERTIFIED remains distinct from convergence.
6. renderer.py: for sphere-rk4, physical PNC and auxiliary RK defects are
   different curves. Auxiliary values are from the saved step's stage audit,
   not a fabricated maximum between saved times. Failed runs explicitly say
   'FAILED: saved frames only'. Legacy integrator labels remain unchanged.

Actual driver checks with the repaired implementation
-----------------------------------------------------
PEN nonadiabatic benchmark, q32[-14,14), g=.01, DSE off, Ntraj=7,
K=1, sphere-rk4 dt=.01 au, support=0, complete 64 internal channels:
* FFT CTMQC and FSSH both reject the proposal after .243339763 fs;
  final saved frames are at .199799845 fs. The auxiliary chart guard,
  not a physical PNC drift, detects the issue.
* FD2 CTMQC and FSSH both complete 1.000208669 fs. PNC <=1.11e-15,
  no masked probability and no hops in this short realization.
  CTMQC energy .24816420 -> .24843246 Ha; N=-.00402362 -> .01244042.
  FSSH expectation energy .24816420 -> .24840277 Ha; N -> .01247116.
  The negative initial N is an unhidden coarse FD2 discretization bias.
  These are NOT matched-H comparisons to the earlier FFT native results.
  They validate the driver, not physical convergence or long-time stability.
* FD2 runs suffered an external SIGTERM at .3996 fs, not a numerical guard.
  Source/config matching was checked by the existing resume implementation;
  the same states/history/RNG continued through .65/.9/1 fs checkpoints.
  This interruption and recovery are separately recorded in result JSON.

Independent fixed-R controls (NOT CTMQC or surface hopping)
----------------------------------------------------------
All use the same declared finite-grid H for Gaussian and uncompressed
reference, including p^2. These isolate the internal compression problem.

* FD2 q64, g=.01, lifted RK4 dt=.02/.01:
  10 fs attempts fail at 2.3526/2.4213 fs with corrupted observables.
* FD2 q64, g=0, dt=.01 and .005:
  both fail at .3744432938 fs. Before failure Gaussian E~8.5923 Ha
  while uncompressed E~.2448276 Ha; Gaussian photon number ~49.
  Physical PNC is still ~1e-15. Halving dt alone is not a repair.
* FD2 q64, g=.01, dt=.01, relative support=1e-6:
  1 fs completes, but N=.862458 vs native .013732,
  E=.389297 vs native .244828 Ha, state L2 discrepancy=.663202.
  Max excluded probability is only 2.664e-7; projection rate residual=.6160.
  Small excluded mass and correct PNC do not imply accurate dynamics.
* K2, q64, FD2, dt=.001: fails near .619815 fs; a larger mixture is not
  automatically sufficient. K3 initialization did not converge within the
  declared fit evaluation budget and was not silently accepted.
* A results-only well-balanced oscillator discretization preserved the
  uncoupled fixed-R stationary control to 10.00015 fs (two grids), but its
  coupled cases still failed or had large observable errors. This different
  finite-grid potential was NOT added to production. Its 10 fs completion
  must NOT be cited as coupled Gaussian/CTMQC/FSSH long-time success.

Interpretation
--------------
The first tail-PNC rejection is a real numerical issue, but removing it does
not establish reliable dynamics. Finite-grid phase evolution, support policy
and finite Gaussian-amplitude projection interact strongly. These tests do
not prove that the continuum P3 formulation is inherently ill-posed. They do
show that time-step reduction, PNC preservation, FD replacement and simple
tail freezing are not a demonstrated 10--40 fs solution for this pilot.
Do not loosen the guards or relabel native dynamics as Gaussian success.
A further method change needs an explicit closure/representation decision
and matched convergence tests before long physical production.
