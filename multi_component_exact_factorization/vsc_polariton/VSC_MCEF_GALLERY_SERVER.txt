INTERPRETATION
q_c is a continuous field quadrature, not photon position, photon number,
or phase angle. Default Q=sqrt(omega_c)*q_c is dimensionless.
rho_Q(Q)dQ is the probability of a quadrature measurement in that interval.
rho_Q=rho_q/sqrt(omega_c); there IS a meaningful photon marginal density.
References: Lacombe et al. PRL123,083201 Eq4; Li et al. NatCommun12,1315 Eq1.

Natural gauge: positive chi(R) and Lambda(q,R).
a=<Phi|-i d_q|Phi>, b=<Phi|-i d_R|Phi>, alpha=<Lambda Phi|-i d_R|Lambda Phi>.
q mass=1, nuclear mass=M. Joint currents j_q=rho_qR*a, j_R=rho_qR*b/M.
Thus arrows on (R,Q) are proportional to (b/M,sqrt(omega)*a), not (b/M,a/mp).
They are instantaneous probability-current directions, NOT photon tracks.
Arrow lengths are normalized for visibility: direction ONLY, not speed magnitude.
Arrow masks use 1e-3 density. Positive squared fields use sequential symlog.
Density/field visualization extends to relative 1e-5, additionally restricted
by the specified excluded-probability budget. Gauge-dependent connections
must not be treated as electromagnetic vector potentials or forces.

The force is -d_R epsilon2 + d_t alpha in this convention. The gallery does
NOT construct an alpha=0 gauge. W_F=-integral F_R dR is a force-work potential
with an anchor within ONE occupied connected component, not an activation
free energy. Undefined/disconnected references remain NaN. Spatial trapezoid
integration/force derivatives require further convergence certification.
The same moving reference is used for the bare comparison. Potential component
means are removed for display, recorded as a convention, not a gauge change.
Large scalar or connection components do not independently prove causality.

Static reference: bare-BO expectation E0 from packet phi0 and native FFT T_x.
Relaxed scalar-DSE BO CBO equals E0 identically. g^2 Var(mu)/w is saved only
as a supplementary projection diagnostic. Neither is a cavity-relaxed full electronic
surface. In full dynamics, photon lag relative to the bare BO valley includes
possible electronic dressing, not solely a temporal response delay.

ONE COMMAND FROM ORIGINAL REAL-GRID WAVES
If fields_*.npz have not been made, replace --fields above with:
  --waves results/vsc_polariton/photon_real_grid/barrier_v1
This calls the existing real-grid diagnostic extractor FIRST, then renders.
NO TDSE propagation is executed. Its working memory is CPU RAM, preferably
12+ GiB free for extraction; rendering from small fields is far cheaper.
No new transfer archive is needed if original packet/waves/observables exist.
Choose a NEW --out on each invocation. Historical data are never overwritten.

Options: --coordinate q (physical q instead of dimensionless Q in new maps)
--density-floor 1e-5 --support-budget 1e-8 --fps 1 --max-frames 1 (smoke only).
Existing reused TDPES panels keep physical q and label it; new maps default Q.
Movie playback uses actual saved frames with timestamps. Unequal physical
time intervals are NOT interpolated into invented intermediate wavefunctions.
