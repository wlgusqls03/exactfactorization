Full-coordinate photon PF runner (2026-09-30)
===========================================

Scope
-----
Independent implementation; historical MCEF/Fock sources are unchanged.
Same Li physical Hamiltonian and same initial packet, including FULL DSE
g_chi^2*(R-x)^2/omega. No fitted PES, BO electronic propagation reduction,
new Gaussian, initial-state retuning, or photon truncation in propagation.

Array: Psi_Q[R,x,Q], Q=sqrt(omega)*q_c, integral |Psi_Q|^2 dR dx dQ = 1.
H = -d_x^2/2 - d_R^2/(2M) - omega*d_Q^2/2
    + V_mol(x,R) + omega/2*[Q+sqrt(2)*g_chi*(R-x)/omega]^2.
All three coordinates use periodic spectral FFT derivatives. The box edges
must remain unoccupied. Physical q-space wave = omega^(1/4)*Psi_Q.
Do NOT feed these new snapshots into an old Fock-to-q converter.

Method and limits
-----------------
Fourth-order Yoshida V/2-T-V/2 composition, three 3D FFT pairs per step.
Potential half-kicks have two cached complex arrays. Kinetic phases are
1D arrays. No large kinetic tensor in propagation and no dense Fock rotation.
Only observe/checkpoint steps transfer small observables / full wave to CPU.
This is a DIFFERENT split from the old exact-H_ph Fock split; dt is audited.

Conservation/boundary limits: norm 1e-9, energy drift 1e-6 Ha,
electron outer 10% strips 1e-8, nuclear outer .3-a0 strips 1e-8,

Hardware gate: full-size instantaneous CPU/GPU H action, 8 identical
CPU/GPU steps (all comparison errors <1e-9), and 128 GPU benchmark steps.
Short hardware PASS is NOT scientific convergence or Phase7 PASS.

Disk, outputs, restart
----------------------
For NR=352,Nx=160, the four-setting complete campaign (pilot + full) forecasts
about 12.74 GiB free including the 2-GiB safety reserve and pending checkpoint.
The pilot alone reserves about 6.78 GiB. No old files are deleted.
384-Q wave: .322 GiB; 512-Q wave: .430 GiB.
Only base full run stores event waves at 0,160,608,672,1408,1440,1652 au.
Refinements/pilots retain initial/final waves; every run has ONE restart.npz.
Observables: every 4 au. Restart: every 64 au + events + requested interruption.
Checkpoint replacement is atomic; previous checkpoint remains until new one
has been flushed. This temporarily needs one extra wave's disk space.
Full wave data are uncompressed for bounded memory/I/O; only return diagnostics
are gzip-compressed. No lossy precision or compression is used.

Re-run the SAME command to resume after a graceful interruption. A completed
run is reused. A FAILED_DIAGNOSTIC is preserved, not resumed into apparent PASS.
Do not remove restart/waves during a running campaign. After a forced kill a
lock can remain: first verify the recorded PID is no longer running; do not
blindly remove a lock from an active process.
